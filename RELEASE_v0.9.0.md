# ALI Simulations v0.9.0 — Session Operations

Adds a usable Belonging Session workspace with role-aware views.

- Participant session home and team-scoped dashboard.
- Partner Needs: any team member may report; administrators verify.
- Contributions: in-kind and monetary entry with numeric quantity/amount/weight; QC verification by Quality Control role or admins.
- Outreach logging with method, outcome and follow-up.
- General activity logging.
- Administrator handoff/impact records.
- Organization/Platform admins see all-team command view; participants see their own team.
- Human-readable audit entries for operational actions.
- Forward-only migration 0007; no reset/truncate/destructive production data operation.
